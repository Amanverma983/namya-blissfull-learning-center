Our Methodology

How We Tailor Each Child's Care Plan

Our holistic clinical process blends multidisciplinary perspectives for the most accurate developmental profile.

01

Initial Consultation & Screening

Deep-dive discussion on child history, gestation milestones, family routines, and teacher observations.

02

Play-Based Clinical Assessment

Direct observation of sensory reactions, hand-eye coordination, speech comprehension, and peer/parent interactions.

03

Structured Therapeutic Plan

Weekly or bi-weekly focused sessions paired with realistic, measurable home-based activity plans for parents.

Unsure which service your child needs?

If you are unsure where to begin, our comprehensive General Pediatric Wellness & Developmental Screening is the best first step. The doctor will evaluate all aspects and recommend the right specialized therapy if needed.
