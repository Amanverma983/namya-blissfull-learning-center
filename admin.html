Slot Management Guidelines

How blocked dates protect the clinic calendar.

  • When a date is marked as All Day Blocked, the public appointment calendar immediately hides all morning and evening slots for that day.
  • When a specific hour is blocked, only that slot will show as reserved/unavailable to public visitors.
  • Existing confirmed appointments are preserved in the list for reference.
